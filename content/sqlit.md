[Speaking of lazygit][2] there’s now the [SQLit][1] TUI for
interacting with SQLite databases.

> The lazygit of SQL databases. Connect to Postgres, MySQL, SQL
> Server, SQLite, Supabase, Turso, and more from your terminal in
> seconds. 
>
> A lightweight TUI for people who just want to run some queries fast.

I’m tempted to see how well it embeds or wraps my personal projects
that happen to have an SQLite DB hiding within them.
