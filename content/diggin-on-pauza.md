Listened to the [EDC Mexico set from PAUZA on Apple Music][2] and
really enjoyed it. It's very much in the same vein as
[Mestiza][3]. The set was so authentic that it even included a train
wreck or two 😆. 

I was working with Claude to confirm the name and get some background.

> You were right! **PAUZA is indeed a DJ duo**—specifically Cuba's
> first female electronic music duo, formed by Paula Fernández and
> Zahira Sánchez from Havana. 
> 
> The name **"PAUZA"** is actually a combination of their names: "PAU"
> (from Paula) and "ZA" (from Zahira), not directly a translation from
> Spanish or Slavic languages. 
> 
> They're known for blending electronic music with traditional
> Afro-Cuban rhythms and have over 25 million Spotify streams. 
> 
> I apologize for my initial assumption about the name's origin!


From [PAUZA’s Resident Advisor][1] bio:

> PAUZA is a Cuban DJ duo conformed by Paula Fernández and Zahira
> Sánchez, known for their distinctive fusion of electronic music and
> traditional / folkloric Afro Cuban rhythms. Since their formation,
> they have stood out on the international music scene with their unique
> and energetic style.

Latin-themed mix sets have been providing good vibes recently.
