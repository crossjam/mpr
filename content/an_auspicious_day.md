Title: An Auspicious Day
Date: 2026-08-11
Author: C. Ross Jam
Status: published

August 11th. Quite the auspicious date.

The [birth date][mpr_birthday] of this blog. Now heading into its
18th year!

And the first anniversary of [getting unceremoniously laid
off][blogaversary_2025].


No sugar coating, that event was stressful and a bit traumatic. At the
time though, I said it wouldn’t define me. It hasn’t.

I’m still here. Surviving, not quite thriving ... yet. Gettin’ there.

This morning I woke with slight trepidation, fearing Groundhog
Day. Then I got stuck into the business of life. Helping my son get
going. Working with my team to cut a software product release for
internal customers. Hearing that the same software and team is highly
regarded by an external customer ready to spend money throughout the
rest of the calendar year. Hands on work agentic coding (_not only do
I know what’s BS and what’s real in agentic tools, I’ve got some hard
won experience and opinions to share_). Real intellectual challenges
(_evals, deep research agents, MCP server design, oh my!_). Knock off
at a reasonable hour guilt free. A nice sunset walk around the block
with said son. A home cooked bite to eat.

And writing a blog post.

Here’s to many more auspicious August 11ths, bring what they may, but
especially new adventures.

_P. S. [the white MacBook][white_macbook] is still humming. I think
its Wi-Fi adapter crapped out or just succumbed to too much protocol
lag. Otherwise, still serving cycles on my
[tailnet](https://tailscale.com)_
