[Pola & Bryson’s mix][1] has been in my rotation for a couple of
months now. Had some highway time today, and it demanded an audience
with CarPlay. It’s of a piece with the work of [DJ Marky][3] and
[Calibre][4]. There’s probably a more fashionable term, but I call it
"Soulful DnB". Definitely drum ’n’ bass at the core: less jump-up, not
much toasting, more soulful vocals, and yes, piano licks _([yellow
shoes][9] ...)_.

> FABRIC MIXES
>
> ## FABRICLIVE presents Pola & Bryson
>
> Rising D&B duo Pola & Bryson launch FABRICLIVE presents, a brand new
> mix and compilation series paying homage to the iconic FABRICLIVE
> series, which ran from 2001 to 2018 and became a defining platform
> for bass music culture in the UK. 
> 
> It includes lead single, ‘Worlds Apart’ ft. Emily Makis, and the
> much-awaited follow-up collaboration since 2023’s ‘Phoneline’. More
> new music comes from Etherwood & Hugh Hardie, Xeonz, Charli Brix and
> Molecular, not forgetting a jam-packed mix featuring heavyweight
> names in D&B such as Camo & Krooked, Mefjus and Breakage, alongside
> newcomers like IYAMAH, Monrroe, and Thread.

Regular readers know I’m in the tank for anything [Fabric
London](https://www.fabriclondon.com). I’ve got a digital version of
all 100 releases for both of [Fabric][5] and [FabricLive][6], plus a
decent collection of the original physical CDs. What I didn’t realize
is that this Pola & Bryson episode is the start of a new mix series. I
had just read it as another release in the [Fabric Presents][7]
series.

Looking forward to seeing more from the renewed FabricLive.
