![Cover image of Fatboy Slim Live At Hï Ibiza, Aug 10, 2022 (DJ Mix)

much more of a [House music][house-music] vibe than [Norman
Cook’s][fatboy-slim] typical [big beat][big-beat] style.

And it’s a damn good mix.
