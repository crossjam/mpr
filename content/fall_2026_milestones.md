Reached some interesting milestones over this past weekend.

## 100 Thousand Scrobbles
Somewhat trivial but not nothing, I passed 100,00 scrobbles in my
[Last.FM library]. That’s over the course of 20+ years, four of which were
empty. I’ll do a post on recent ownership changes at Last.FM but I get
the sense that there isn’t a large population with that longevity and
usage.

![A screen capture from Last.FM illustrating that I have over 100,000
scrobbles logged in my pro account]({static}/images/LastFM 100 Thousand.png)


## fabric and FABRICLIVE mix completion

While [discogs.com](https://discogs.com) is my goto for [fabric][2]
and [FABRICLIVE][3] release data, Wikipedia has [a nice compact
listing][4] of all the releases in one place.

I’ve been having some fun taking my [scrobbledb] project, deploying it
onto [exe.dev], and using [shelley] on my phone to interrogate my
automatically synced scrobbles (_thanks to good old cron_). Definitely
a project which deserves a series of posts.

A week or two ago, I did a quick natural language query to see how
many mixes in each series **had not** been scrobbled yet. Turned out,
six apiece. To be honest, I knew for a fact that I actually had
listened to many of those that were outstanding. But I thought, what
the heck, let’s just knock this out. So I did!

To the extent that scrobbles are a reliable marker, there is evidence
that I have listened to all 200 fabric and FABRICLIVE mixes. Next up
_fabric presents_.

## Six Months of Employment

About two and a half weeks back, I passed the six month mark in a full
time work engagement. I’m generally going to low profile details
because 1) opsec (_national security stuff_) and 2) identity (_as in I
am not a defined by a job_).

I hinted at it when I posted about [an auspicious day], but never
really acknowledged that I’ve got a full time gig. The work has been
intellectually challenging, the people enjoyable, and the company
interesting. Not that getting laid off was fortunate, but the timing
allowed me to have some slack to get more engaged with coding agents
just as they really started to become effective circa late 2025 (_has
it only been that long_). The day job enterprise done drank the
agentic coding kool-aid and so I’ve been stuck into it for six months
and **really** deep in the weeds for the better part of three. More to
come.
