While crate diggin’ on Apple Music, I landed on the highly listenable
[Gudu Mix Series][1]

> GUDU
>
> Founded by Peggy Gou in 2019, Gudu Records releases cutting-edge contemporary
> electronic music from a global roster of artists such as Mogwaa, Maurice
> Fulton, DMX Krew, Dea, and Peggy herself.
>
> This page collects the archive to Gudu’s popular DJ mix series, with
> contributions from label family and friends alike including Salamanda, Kim Ann
> Foxman, Chae, fka.m4a, and more.

The few mixes I’ve listened to in full lean toward ’90s and early
2000s Chicago and New York [tech house][2]. I need to sample a few
more mixes to get a sense of the overall oeuvre. Looking promising
though.
